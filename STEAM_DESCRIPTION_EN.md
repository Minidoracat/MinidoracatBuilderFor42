[h1]🏗️ Minidoracat Build Anything[/h1]
[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]

[hr][/hr]

Build with any tile in the game, including other mods' tile packs: walls, floors, doors and windows, stairs, roofs, furniture, lights and light switches. You pay for what you build with materials.

[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Required:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789836701]Minidoracat UI Library[/url]
[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819478078]Minidoracat Safehouse[/url] (its safehouse permissions decide where you can build)
[*] Works in singleplayer and multiplayer; in multiplayer the server must enable this mod
[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] can be added mid-save; read the full guide before removing it. Everything you built stays after removal, but ground floors can no longer be removed, and in multiplayer the switches and non-movable lights built with Build Anything inside houses you built yourself stop responding
[*] [b]Languages:[/b] 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Français, Polski, Deutsch (please report any translation issues)
[/list]

[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Click the Build Anything button on the family toolbar to open the build window
[*] Find what you want with search or the tabs, select it and press Build
[*] Click the ground to place it; hold the left button and drag to build a row of walls or floors
[*] Keep the materials in your bags or on the ground next to you; the details list anything missing
[/olist]

[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Every loaded tile[/b]: vanilla and tile pack mods in one catalog, with search and favorites
[*] [b]Vanilla-style costs[/b]: materials follow vanilla dismantling or building, with no skill level needed and XP for building
[*] [b]Drag a row[/b]: walls, floors, fences and roofs in one drag, skipping tiles you can't afford
[*] [b]Switches for the lights you pick[/b]: choose which lights each switch controls, movable lights included
[*] [b]Auto wiring and two-way switches[/b]: a switch in a closed room wires itself to the lights there, and two switches can share them
[*] [b]Dismantle[/b]: right-click what you built and choose Dismantle (Build Anything) to get materials back
[*] [b]Server-checked[/b]: the server checks materials, distance and safehouse permissions
[*] [b]Admin settings[/b]: who can build, where, the material multiplier, and turning off tiles from specific mods
[/list]
