[h1]🏗️ Minidoracat Build Anything[/h1]
[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]

[hr][/hr]

Build with any tile in the game, including other mods' tile packs: walls, floors, doors and windows, stairs, roofs, furniture, lights and light switches. You pay for what you build with materials.

[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Required:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789836701]Minidoracat UI Library[/url]
[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819478078]Minidoracat Safehouse[/url] (its safehouse permissions decide where you can build)
[*] Works in singleplayer and multiplayer; in multiplayer the server must enable this mod
[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] can be added mid-save; read the full guide before removing it. Everything you built stays after removal, but ground floors and floor overlays can no longer be removed, and in multiplayer the switches and non-movable lights built with Build Anything inside houses you built yourself stop responding
[*] [b]Languages:[/b] 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Français, Polski, Deutsch (please report any translation issues)
[/list]

[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Click the Build Anything button on the family toolbar to open the build window
[*] Find what you want with search or the tabs, select it and press Build
[*] Click the ground to place it; hold the left button and drag to build a row or a whole area
[*] Keep the materials in your bags or on the ground next to you; the details list anything missing
[*] For a whole house, press Plan in the window to draw a blueprint on the ground, then press Build from blueprint and your character builds it
[/olist]

[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Every loaded tile[/b]: vanilla and tile pack mods in one searchable catalog, with the vanilla build menu's recipes, garage doors, double doors and ramps listed too
[*] [b]Vanilla-style costs[/b]: materials follow vanilla dismantling or building and give XP, with optional hardcore skill levels and recipe consumables
[*] [b]Drag rows and areas[/b]: walls and fences go around a rectangle, floors and roofs fill it, skipping tiles you can't afford and splitting big drags into batches; R turns walls, doors and windows to any edge of a tile
[*] [b]Blueprints[/b]: plan a whole building on the ground as a ghost that costs nothing, then let your character build it batch by batch, fetching what's short from supply boxes you pick; the Roof Helper lays out a whole roof over an area you drag; share blueprints as a code or capture an existing building
[*] [b]Quick pick bar and keys[/b]: while placing, recent items and the full set sit at the bottom of the screen; the eyedropper, overlap and undo last build have keys
[*] [b]Dig basements (off by default)[/b]: once the server turns on "Basements" in the "what can be built" sandbox page, place a "Basement Entrance" on the ground floor to open wooden stairs down, then use "Dig This Tile" on the level below to dig outward. Only a shovel is needed (the entrance also takes the materials for wooden stairs); dirt walls form around what you dig, it counts as indoors, and you can swap the dirt walls for other walls. One level down only, and dug tiles can't be filled back in; with Basement Building installed, dig with that mod instead. Known issue: underground, moving the vanilla "Dig Graves" cursor past the walls or moving multi-tile furniture prints vanilla errors in the console
[*] [b]Switches and wiring[/b]: choose which lights each switch controls, or let a switch in a closed room wire itself; two switches can share lights
[*] [b]Dismantle and swap[/b]: right-click what you built, or drag over an area, to remove it and get materials back (it doesn't replace the vanilla sledgehammer); build another style over your own floor or wall and pay only the difference
[*] [b]Server-checked, admin-controlled[/b]: the server checks materials, distance and safehouse permissions; admins turn kinds, mods and tilesets on or off in game and change the building rules
[/list]
