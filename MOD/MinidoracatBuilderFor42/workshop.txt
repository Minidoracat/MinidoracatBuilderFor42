description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=Build with any tile in the game, including other mods' tile packs: walls, floors, doors and windows, stairs, roofs, furniture, lights and light switches. You pay for what you build with materials.
description=
description=[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Required:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789836701]Minidoracat UI Library[/url]
description=[*] [b]Optional:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819478078]Minidoracat Safehouse[/url] (its safehouse permissions decide where you can build)
description=[*] Works in singleplayer and multiplayer; in multiplayer the server must enable this mod
description=[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] can be added mid-save; read the full guide before removing it. Everything you built stays after removal, but ground floors can no longer be removed, and in multiplayer the switches and non-movable lights built with Build Anything inside houses you built yourself stop responding
description=[*] [b]Languages:[/b] 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Français, Polski, Deutsch (please report any translation issues)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Click the Build Anything button on the family toolbar to open the build window
description=[*] Find what you want with search or the tabs, select it and press Build
description=[*] Click the ground to place it; hold the left button and drag to build a row of walls or floors
description=[*] Keep the materials in your bags or on the ground next to you; the details list anything missing
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Every loaded tile[/b]: vanilla and tile pack mods in one searchable catalog, or right-click something on the ground to pick its tile
description=[*] [b]Vanilla-style costs[/b]: materials follow vanilla dismantling or building, with no skill level needed and XP for building
description=[*] [b]Drag a row[/b]: walls, floors, fences and roofs in one drag, skipping tiles you can't afford
description=[*] [b]Switches for the lights you pick[/b]: choose which lights each switch controls, movable lights included
description=[*] [b]Auto wiring and two-way switches[/b]: a switch in a closed room wires itself to the lights there, and two switches can share them
description=[*] [b]Dismantle[/b]: right-click what you built and choose Dismantle (Build Anything) to get materials back
description=[*] [b]Server-checked[/b]: the server checks materials, distance and safehouse permissions
description=[*] [b]Admin settings[/b]: turn kinds, mods and tilesets on or off in game, and change the building rules
description=[/list]
